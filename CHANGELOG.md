# Changelog

## v1.1.0

Optimized embedded Skill release. The eight selected embedded Skills were packaged and verified from the local `skills1` source; only `embedded-code-review` differs from v1.0.0.

- Refined the review workflow to focus on reachable execution paths, selective context gathering, evidence-based candidate refutation, and explicit coverage limits.
- Expanded the supporting review evidence for buffer/protocol boundaries, RTOS/ISR ownership, DMA/MMIO, Flash/OTA recovery, and targeted validation.
- Clarified read-only review behavior and reporting requirements to reduce unsupported or generic findings.

Original version. Preserved unchanged at the original `main` commit.
